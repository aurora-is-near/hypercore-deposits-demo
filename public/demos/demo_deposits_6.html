How to integrate

Swap API — bridge, then your existing zap

  1. Add one tab. Everything behind it is a quote plus your existing deposit — no new page, no router change.
  2. Build the token list from GET /supported-chains and GET /supported-tokens so new assets appear without a release.
  3. Step 1 · Bridge. POST /quote with the origin asset, destination = the pool asset on BNB Chain, recipient = the user's own address. The response carries the rate, fees and a single-use depositAddress.
  4. Offer both inputs at that step: on an EVM chain the user signs from their connected wallet; on any non-EVM chain they send to the depositAddress, rendered as a QR. Same address either way.
  5. Poll GET /status until funds land, then enable step 2.
  6. Step 2 · Deposit calls your existing zapper entrypoint, confirmed by the connected BNB Chain wallet. The ratio split and the position stay entirely in Thena's code.

Stays exactly the same

    Two ways in

    • Connected wallet — any EVM chain. No bridging, no BNB for gas.
    • QR deposit address — every non-EVM chain (Solana, Sui, BTC, Tron, TON, NEAR…), and any funds sitting on an exchange. Plain transfer, auto-detected.
    • Both are the same step 1 — the bridge. Step 2 is then a plain BNB Chain deposit.
